# Privacy Policy — Pitakap

**Last updated: 9 September 2026**

Pitakap ("the app") is a personal expense, bills and subscription tracker. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The app is operated by the developer of Pitakap ("we", "us"). Contact: **[ADD A CONTACT EMAIL BEFORE PUBLISHING]**

## What we collect

Everything below is collected only because the app cannot work without it. **There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.**

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | To create and sign in to your account | Firebase Authentication |
| First and last name | To personalise the app (e.g. the dashboard greeting) | Cloud Firestore |
| Account identifier | To attach your records to your account | Firebase Authentication / Cloud Firestore |
| Your subscriptions | The records you create: name, category, amount, billing cycle, dates, reminder lead time, notes | Cloud Firestore |
| Your expenses | The records you create: description, category, amount, payment method, date | Cloud Firestore |
| Your preferences | Default currency, default reminder lead time, light/dark theme | Stored **only on your device** |

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

## What we do not collect

- No advertising identifiers, and no advertising SDKs.
- No analytics or usage tracking. There is no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or equivalent in the app.
- No location data.
- No contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access.
- No payment card numbers or bank credentials. Pitakap does **not** connect to your bank. "Payment method" is a label you type yourself (for example "Cash" or "GCash") — it is not an account number.

## How your data is used

Only to run the app for you: to authenticate you, to store and display the records you create, to calculate your totals and category breakdowns, and to schedule renewal reminders on your device.

**We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for advertising or any other purpose.**

## Where your data is stored

Your records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the **asia-southeast1 (Singapore)** region, and your account is held in Firebase Authentication. Both are operated by Google. Google's handling of that data is governed by the [Firebase Privacy and Security policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

Access is enforced per account by server-side security rules: each account can only read and write its own records.

The app also keeps a local copy of your data on your device so it works offline. That copy is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

## Notifications

Renewal reminders are scheduled **on your device**. Nothing about your subscriptions is sent to a notification server, and no push notifications are sent to you from us. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device settings.

## Your choices and rights

- **See your data** — everything the app holds about you is visible in the app itself.
- **Correct it** — edit or delete any subscription or expense at any time.
- **Delete everything** — Settings → **Delete account** permanently removes your account and its records. This cannot be undone.
- **Withdraw consent** — stop using the app and delete your account.

If you are in a jurisdiction with statutory data rights (for example the EU/UK GDPR, or the Philippines' Data Privacy Act of 2012), you may also contact us at the address above to exercise them.

## Children

Pitakap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Retention

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. There is no separate archive: deleting the account deletes the records.
